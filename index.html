From the Desk of Our HOD

A Message of Welcome & Vision

"Hospitality is not merely a profession — it is the art of making every human being feel valued, seen, and at home. At IIIHM, we don't just teach that art; we live it every day."

Dear aspiring hospitality professionals and future leaders of the industry — welcome to IIIHM, IEM's International Institute of Hotel Management. As the Head of Department, it is my honour and privilege to welcome you into a community that has, for over two decades, been at the forefront of hospitality education in Eastern India.

The hospitality industry is evolving at an unprecedented pace — driven by globalisation, technology, and a deep shift in what guests expect from a service experience. At IIIHM, our academic philosophy is rooted in preparing you not just for the industry as it is today, but for the industry as it will be tomorrow. Our programmes — BHMCT and BHHA — are carefully crafted to blend rigorous academic foundations with hands-on, real-world immersion.

I encourage every student who walks through our doors to embrace curiosity, develop resilience, and cultivate the spirit of service. The world's finest hotels are built not on grand architecture alone, but on the character of the people within them. You are that character. Come, let us build it together.

Prof. Koustav Bose
Head of Department, IIIHM
IEM's International Institute of Hotel Management, Kolkata
